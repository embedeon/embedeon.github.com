Embedded systems, designed as a whole.

Embedeon does research and development for embedded systems: firmware, electronics, housings and the software around them, worked out together from the first prototype to a device that runs.

What we do

Engineering for your project, or for our own products, from the first idea to a tested device.

Embedded firmware

Bare-metal and RTOS firmware, drivers, display interfaces and desktop simulators for testing firmware without hardware.

Electronics and PCB design

Schematics, circuit simulation, board layout and ready-to-order assembly files.

Enclosures and mechanical parts

Housings designed in CAD around the board, 3D-printed prototypes and product renders.

Bring-up, test and review

First power-on, automated tests, and an independent design review before anything is ordered.

Apps and web software

Companion apps, web tools and back ends that work with the device.

AI-assisted engineering

AI agents do much of the design work, and automated checks and a separate reviewer check every result. That makes us fast without skipping steps, and we can set up the same workflow for your team.
